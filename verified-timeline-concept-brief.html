Concept Brief

Verified Timeline & Claim Checker

A real-time, India-focused system that shows confirmed updates during fast-moving events, and lets anyone check a claim they saw elsewhere against verified sources.

01 — The problem

Confirmed information can't keep up with rumor

During protests, elections, and fast-moving political events, false and half-verified information spreads faster than confirmed updates. Mainstream news often arrives late and isn't always trusted either, so people default to scrolling Instagram, Reddit, and X, platforms where the algorithm decides what's shown, influencers add opinion without accountability, and there's no way to separate confirmed fact from rumor in real time.

The same gap exists for global geopolitics and conflicts that directly affect people in India, with no single place to see what's actually confirmed as events unfold.

02 — The solution

One verification engine, two front doors

Not a chatbot wrapper. A real data pipeline: ingest from trusted sources, classify each item, store it, then serve it two ways. This mirrors the published architecture of ClaimBuster, a real production fact-checking system, claim monitor → claim spotter → claim matcher.

The LLM's only job is the last step on the right: turning already-retrieved, already-verified evidence into a plain-language explanation. It never answers from its own unverified knowledge, which is what separates this from a chatbot wrapper.

03 — Data sources

What feeds the engine

PIB RSS + Fact Check Telegram Live

Official government releases plus PIB's Fact Check Unit, which already labels claims Fake, Misleading, or confirmed after checking against official sources. Covers only Government of India-related claims.

ACLED Weekly

Free after registration. Covers protests and conflict events globally, including India, fills the gap PIB's narrow mandate leaves open. Updates weekly, labeled honestly as such in the UI.

X / Reddit direct ingestion Not used

No free API access as of 2026 for either platform, and ingesting raw social posts would reintroduce the same unverified, algorithm-driven content this project exists to fix.

Verified official accounts on X Roadmap

Monitoring specific official handles (police, ministries) is legitimate and ClaimBuster-style, but still needs paid API access. Scoped as a labeled Phase 2 item, not built now.

Newspaper model, not algorithm model

No inferred personalization. Two separate, transparent mechanisms instead of one black-box feed.

08 — Decision log

Why it's built this way

Q. Why not just a chatbot?

A wrapper answers from an LLM's own training. This retrieves real, verified evidence first and only uses the LLM to explain it, the same retrieval-augmented pattern ClaimBuster uses in production.

Q. Why not personalize the feed like social media does?

Inferred personalization is the exact mechanism the project is built to fix. Newspaper-style opt-in topics plus transparent, identical-for-everyone ranking avoids rebuilding the same problem.

Q. Why not pull directly from X or Reddit?

Neither has free API access in 2026, and raw social posts aren't verified. The reactive checker already covers "I saw this on social media", the user pastes it, no ingestion needed.

Q. Why PIB and ACLED specifically?

Both are real, free or low-barrier, and structured. PIB's mandate only covers Government of India claims, which is exactly why ACLED is needed to cover protests and conflict.

Q. Why build working software when only a concept is due?

Most teams will submit slides only. A working thin-slice demo proves the hardest technical claim, that the engine actually works, and stands out immediately.

Q. Which track do we submit under?

AI & ML. The retrieval, classification, and matching logic is the core innovation, even though the problem itself touches current affairs and civic information.
