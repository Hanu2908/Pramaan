01 — Sharper problem statement

Not just crisis response, a daily habit problem

The original brief framed this around fast-moving events like protests and geopolitics. The real starting point is more everyday: wanting to follow the news without the time to actively hunt for it, not trusting interest-based algorithmic feeds, and, like many young people, ending up sourcing news informally from platforms like Instagram that were never built for it.

This is the stronger pitch. It's relatable before it's civic, and it directly justifies why the product has to update automatically rather than wait for the user to go looking.

02 — Two-lane proactive model

Not every story needs verification

Some news is an undisputed fact from an authoritative primary source. Some news is a claim that needs checking. Treating both the same way was over-engineering the easy half and under-engineering the hard half.

Direct Record
ISRO, PIB releases, NewsData.io — shown as-is with attribution
Verified Claims
protests, communal, political, health — full pipeline

Example: a rocket launch needs a source, not a verification pipeline. A claim about clashes at a protest needs both.

03 — Full source list

Broadened, and role-sorted

PIB RSS Live

Government of India press releases, all ministries. Direct Record for policy/government activity.

Alt News RSS Near-daily

Independent fact-checks of viral social, communal, and political claims, including AI-generated media. Closes the gap PIB's government-only mandate leaves.

ACLED Weekly

Structured protest and conflict event data. Repositioned as a corroboration layer, not the primary freshness source.

NewsData.io Live

Aggregates thousands of publishers, including Indian outlets like Indian Express and Mint, across all topics. Free tier permits deployed/demo use. Powers the Direct Record lane and tags general headlines "developing" until corroborated.

Reality Defender On-demand

Synthetic image and audio detection, free tier 50/month. Video detection not yet available on the free API, stays Phase 2.

Gemini Grounding Fallback only

Used only when nothing above returns a match. Always labeled explicitly as an unofficial, lower-confidence web signal.

04 — The matching engine

One engine, two triggers

Powers both the reactive checker (user submits a claim) and the proactive timeline (a new item auto-checks itself against existing evidence and upgrades its own confidence tier). Not two systems, one pipeline with two entry points.

Stage 1
Input normalization
Text, image (Groq vision OCR extracts embedded text), or audio (Groq Whisper transcribes). Everything becomes claim_text, with a synthetic_score attached if media was involved.
Stage 2
Entity extraction — real NLP stage
Structured-output call to Groq extracts location, date range, actors, and topic category into a fixed JSON schema.
Stage 3
Structured candidate filtering
SQL filter against the pre-tagged Supabase evidence store using the extracted entities. Cheap, fast, no ML cost.
Stage 4
Semantic re-ranking — real ML stage
Embed claim_text (Gemini embeddings), compare via cosine similarity against candidate embeddings in Supabase pgvector. Rank, take top matches above threshold.
Stage 5
Confidence scoring
Combine source agreement, similarity score, source authority, and recency into Confirmed / Developing / Unverified / No Record Found.
Stage 6
Fallback
Only if Stage 4 returns nothing above threshold: escalate to Gemini grounding, labeled unofficial.
Stage 7
Constrained synthesis
Groq explains the matched evidence in plain language, instructed to cite sources by name and never state anything the evidence doesn't support.

06 — Corrections and honest calls

Worth stating plainly, not glossing over

Q. Why isn't PIB Fact Check enough on its own?

It's a government body fact-checking public claims, including claims critical of government, which has a real, documented one-sidedness critique. Requiring agreement with an independent source (Alt News) before the highest confidence tier turns this from a weakness into a stated design principle.

Q. Is Groq the same as Grok / xAI?

No. Groq is an unrelated inference company (custom chips, hosts open-weight models like Llama and Whisper). It has no access to X data and cannot search X. Caught before it became a factual error in the concept doc.

Q. Why not build our own text-based fake news classifier?

Media forensics works because generated images and audio leave detectable artifacts. Fake text has no equivalent reliable signal, truth isn't a stylistic property. A black-box classifier would undercut the evidence-grounded approach this whole project is built on.

Q. Does deepfake detection cover video?

Not this week. Reality Defender's free API currently covers image and audio only. Video detection stays a labeled Phase 2 roadmap item, same treatment as official X-account monitoring.
